Tested environments and limits

Choose Fedora 44 or 45 and the matching CPU. amd64 package revision 2 and ARM64 revision 1 have different verification records. amd64 used WSL2/WSLg; ARM64 used Xorg VMs.

Physical hardware, other OS versions, Wayland and Japanese IME coverage differ by artifact. Support for every desktop environment is not established. See each Release and the installation guide for detailed results.

Install, launch and update

  • Open the matching OS/CPU/version Release, obtain the package and SHA256SUMS.txt, and verify SHA-256.
  • Follow that Release’s dependency and installation instructions. Launch the app as a regular user.
  • After installation, launch using folimeld.
  • Back up PDFs and settings before updating or removing the app, and check the instructions for that format.
