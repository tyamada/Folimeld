Folimeld

Edit PDFs with visual page previews

A desktop app for rearranging, rotating, inserting, and deleting PDF pages. PDFs and images are processed on your device without being sent to external services.

Available for Windows, macOS, Ubuntu, and Debian, with preview releases for FreeBSD, NetBSD, and OpenBSD.

See the English guide for supported OS versions, CPUs, package formats, installation, and launch instructions. Source archives and SHA-256 checksums are available in each Release.
